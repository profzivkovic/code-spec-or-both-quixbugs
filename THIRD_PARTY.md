# Third-party material

## QuixBugs

This repository vendors the QuixBugs benchmark under:

```text
external/QuixBugs/
```

Upstream repository:

https://github.com/jkoppel/QuixBugs

QuixBugs is a benchmark of 40 programs from the Quixey Challenge, with buggy implementations and corrected Python versions.

The vendored QuixBugs directory preserves the upstream `LICENSE`, `README.md`, and `legal_notes.txt`.

The upstream `LICENSE` is an MIT-style license with copyright attributed to James Koppel (2017–2019). The upstream legal notes also document the benchmark authors' discussion of permission to redistribute the original Quixey Challenge material.

The root `LICENSE` of this research repository applies to original code and research-artifact material authored for this study. It does not override licenses, notices, or rights associated with third-party material under `external/QuixBugs/`.
